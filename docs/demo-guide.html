ParkSpace: Demo Guide

Every flow, screen by screen, with what you're seeing and what to say.

Proof-of-concept build · captured on a phone-sized screen · UK launch market (GBP) · Working name: ParkSpace (placeholder brand)

How to use this guide

The demo follows nine short flows, about 25 minutes in total, or pick the ones that matter to your audience. Each card shows the screen, what you are seeing, and a suggested line to say. The coloured tag shows who is logged in: Driver, Host or Admin.

Before you start

  1. Open the app: https://saeedmurrad.github.io/driveway-parking-app/
  2. Wake the backend about a minute earlier by opening https://parkspace-api.onrender.com/health. The free hosting tier sleeps when idle.
  3. Use two browser windows (one normal, one private) so you can be the driver and the host at the same time.
  4. Use the demo role buttons on the login screen, or the accounts below. Password for all: demo1234.

Good to know

  • Payments are simulated, and email, SMS and push messages are shown in-app. Demo mode shows verification codes on screen.
  • The dispute window is set to 2 minutes so host payouts can be shown live. The spec's default is 24 hours.
  • On a free tier the first load after a quiet spell can take about a minute.

Wrap-up talking points

  • Reliability first: the instant refund when a space is blocked, host-cancellation refunds and a disputes desk answer the biggest complaints about competing apps.
  • Beyond booking: price negotiation, paid extras (including EV charging matched to the car), request-to-book and overstay handling are built and working.
  • Money you can trust: every total comes from a payment ledger, the commission rate is stored per booking, and every admin action is logged.
  • Decision for stakeholders: the planned 20% host commission is higher than JustPark's reported host fee. See the competitor analysis for options.
  • Next steps: real Stripe payments, real email/SMS/push, insurance and legal terms, and a launch-area supply plan.
